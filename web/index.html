Four steps and it's runningCuatro pasos y está funcionando

  1. Run the installer. It generates its own keys — you never invent one.
  2. Choose your dashboard password.
  3. Scan the QR with WhatsApp.
  4. Point your agent at it, over MCP.
  1. Ejecuta el instalador. Genera solo sus claves: nunca inventas ninguna.
  2. Elige la contraseña de tu tablero.
  3. Escanea el QR con WhatsApp.
